# Blind review of paired signing motions

Open `reviewer_sheet.csv`. Each row has a paired video with A and B in synchronized columns and front and side views in rows. The separate RGB reference shows the real signing. English text is approximate context. Use the signed video to resolve its actual content.

For each pair, judge separately:

- **Handshape.** Which candidate better preserves the visible finger configuration and wrist orientation that matter for the reference sign? Enter A, B, tie, or unclear.
- **Meaning.** Which candidate better preserves intended sign identity or understandability? Enter A, B, tie, or unclear. Use unclear if the skeletal display hides essential cues.
- **Confidence.** Use a 1–5 scale and optionally note the time window and the cue driving the choice. Ties are allowed.

Each reviewer should fill a separate copy independently. A/B ordering is randomized. Keep all experiment scores and the external identity key away from reviewers until ratings are collected.

Source RGB is from How2Sign, released for non-commercial research under CC BY-NC 4.0. ASL annotations and fitted-motion provenance are in the experiment documentation. [How2Sign](https://how2sign.github.io/)
